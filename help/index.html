Help center

Accounts

Sign-in keeps your username, Birg Bucks, cosmetics, and progress on the server. Guests are browser-local only. For password or lock issues, email support with your username (locks escalate).

Birg Bucks

In-game play money for the Shop. Earn via coins, building (daily cap), daily bonus, obbies/rewards, and events. Pack buttons are DEMO — no real charges yet.

Iceberg Plus

Planned at $9/mo (badge, Mobile Studio custom colors, Studio custom prefabs). The UI says Coming soon — nothing is charged; purchases are not live.

Bans

Only the developer (Colin, account Gooberr478) can ban or unban, using developer mode (type a username).

A ban locks that account and its device out of every game, chat, and multiplayer — even if someone makes a new account on the same device. There is no mute tier yet; a ban is a full lock.

Appeals: email iceberggames@mail.grokbot.com with your username and what happened. Customer Service drafts a note for Colin; only he can unban. Bans stay in place until Colin unbans (no timed bans).

Bugs

Please include your device, browser, game, steps to reproduce, and a screenshot if you can.

Lag tips: close extra tabs, use Chrome or Edge, hard refresh, or try another network.

How to play

Start → pick a game → Play. WASD / Space / mouse to move; B to build; T / L / M / H for UI; F with the Ice Rocket to fly.

Studio on computer: Create, Test, Save, Publish.
