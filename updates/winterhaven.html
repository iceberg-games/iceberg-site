What's coming

Explore a cozy snowy village at night. Winterhaven is an all-original Iceberg event — its map, music and sound effects were made by the Iceberg team.

What you'll do

  • Help the villagers of Winterhaven with their quests
  • Climb to Frostspire Castle
  • Find Glimmer Cave
  • Race the Frostgate Run on frozen Mirrorfrost Lake
  • Earn limited-time Winterhaven cosmetics during the event

For parents

Winterhaven is a gentle, cozy adventure: exploring a snowy village, helping characters, and a friendly race on a frozen lake. The event runs Oct 25 – Nov 1, 2026 (America/Phoenix). Questions? Visit our Help center.
